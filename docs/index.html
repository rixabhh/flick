CLEAR PRIVACY CONTROLS

Your words stay
under your control.

01

You choose the context

Replies consider only the text you select. Flick doesn't read your screen and keeps no conversation history.

02

Insert is target-checked

Switch apps mid-draft and Flick withholds the paste — the finished text waits in the composer and on your clipboard, never in the wrong window.

03

Keys live in the OS keychain

Provider keys are stored by your operating system — never in prefs files, never logged. Cloud dictation only ever after opt-in, and every transcription run is visible.
